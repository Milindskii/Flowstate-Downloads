# 🦊 Flowstate

**Flowstate** is a calm, personal productivity companion built around your natural energy rhythms, accompanied by your focus companion **Noya**.

Rather than managing rigid, stressful to-do lists, Flowstate helps you balance deep work with restorative recovery so you can stay consistent without burnout.

---

### ✨ Key Features

- **Energy-Rhythm Scheduling:** Automatically plans your most demanding tasks during peak energy windows and places rest breaks during natural recovery dips.
- **Noya Companion:** An interactive presence that tracks your daily flow, celebrates completed commitments, and keeps you encouraged.
- **Distraction-Free Focus Rooms:** Built-in ambient soundscapes and gentle timers to help you enter and sustain focus sessions.
- **Streak & Shield Protection:** Earn streak freeze shields through focus habits and daily consistency to protect your progress when life happens.
- **Flow Hub:** Track your personal progress, manage rewards, and access focus tools.
